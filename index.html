INBOX · STEP TWO04 / 15

Inbox step 2: teach it with your sent emails.

SAY THIS

Look at the last 300 to 500 emails I've sent and understand three things.

  • First, the categories of emails I reply to.
  • Second, for each category, my literature fingerprint: my tone, length, greeting, structure, and the words I use often.
  • Third, for each category, my reasoning and context: how I decide what to say, and what files or information I gather before I reply.

Then interview me. Ask 3 to 5 questions, one at a time, and use each answer to shape your next question. Find out anything else you need, including which emails I don't reply to and why.

Then turn everything you learned into a skill. When I run it, it should find emails I haven't replied to that fit one of my categories, draft a reply to each one in my voice, and save each reply in my email drafts folder, not in this chat. Write only the body of the email and skip the signature, because my email adds it for me.

SETUP TWO · REVIEWING DOCUMENTS08 / 15

Documents: teach AI the lens you review with.

DOCUMENTS · YOUR PAST REVIEWS09 / 15

Documents: show it three you've reviewed.

SAY THIS

Here are three [type of document] I've reviewed, along with the comments I made on each one. Methodically reverse engineer how I review these: what I check first, what I push back on, what I ignore, and why.

Then interview me with [number] questions, one at a time, using each answer to shape your next question, so you understand the thinking behind my comments that isn't written down.

When we're done, turn this into a skill that reviews the next one the way I would. For each point, quote the part of the document it's about.

DOCUMENTS · LAST TIME10 / 15

Documents: compare it with the last version.

SAY THIS

Here's this quarter's report and last quarter's. What changed? What's missing this time? What did they say they would do last time that they don't mention now?

SETUP THREE · A SPARRING PARTNER11 / 15

Decisions: by default, AI agrees with you.

DECISIONS · STEP ONE12 / 15

Decisions: give AI one place to push back.

DECISIONS · STEP TWO13 / 15

Decisions: tell it exactly how to push back.

THE PROJECT OR FOLDER INSTRUCTIONS

This is where I test big decisions before I make them.

When I share a plan, red team it: tell me how it could fail, what I'm missing, and what a smart person who disagrees would say.

The answer is for me, the person who has to make this call and live with it, so be useful, not kind.

If I push back without giving you new facts, keep your view and explain why.
